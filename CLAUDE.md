### Quick Reference:
- **`raw/`**: Read-only source documents (broker statements, trade logs, book chapters, screenshots).
- **`wiki/`**: Persistent, cross-referenced knowledge base maintained by you (the LLM).
- **`index.md`**: Up-to-date catalog of all pages. Always update upon creating pages.
- **`log.md`**: Append-only ledger formatted with `## [YYYY-MM-DD] <operation> | <Title>`.
- **Primary Goal**: Help the trader systematically improve edge, eliminate emotional tilt, enforce strict risk management, and compound trading wisdom over time.
