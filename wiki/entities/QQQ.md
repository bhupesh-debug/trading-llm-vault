# Entity Profile: QQQ (Nasdaq-100 ETF)

The dominant benchmark for US high-beta technology, AI mega-caps, and momentum growth.

---

## 1. Key Characteristics
- **Beta / Volatility**: Higher beta than [[SPY]]. Larger ATR in dollar and percentage terms, providing faster moves for day traders.
- **Top Holdings**: Heavily weighted towards Semiconductor and Megacap Tech (NVDA, MSFT, AAPL, AMZN, META, AVGO).
- **Macro Drivers**: Long-duration yield sensitivity, semiconductor earnings cycles, semiconductor supply chain data.

---

## 2. Intraday Personality & Execution Notes
- **Clean Momentum**: When mega-cap tech is trending in unison, QQQ offers strong continuation breakouts.
- **Fakeouts in Chop**: When semis and software diverge, QQQ can produce sharp stop-runs around key moving averages.
- **Strategy Compatibility**:
  - [[Opening_Range_Breakout]] during earnings season.
  - [[Support_Resistance_Liquidity_Sweep]] at PMH/PML levels.
