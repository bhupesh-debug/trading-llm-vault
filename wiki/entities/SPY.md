# Entity Profile: SPY (S&P 500 ETF)

The bellwether benchmark for US equity market sentiment, liquidity, and systemic trend.

---

## 1. Key Characteristics
- **Trading Hours**: Regular Trading Hours (RTH) 09:30 - 16:00 EST. Extended hours 04:00 - 20:00 EST.
- **Top Holdings**: Microsoft (MSFT), Apple (AAPL), Nvidia (NVDA), Amazon (AMZN), Alphabet (GOOGL), Meta (META).
- **Macro Drivers**: Federal Reserve interest rate expectations, 10-Year US Treasury Yields (`US10Y`), CPI/PPI inflation data, corporate earnings season.

---

## 2. Intraday Personality & Execution Notes
- **Reversals vs Trends**: SPY tends to respect session VWAP closely. When trading within ±0.5% of VWAP, expect range oscillations and liquidity sweeps.
- **Key Levels**: Always plot Prior Day High (PDH), Prior Day Low (PDL), Prior Day Close (PDC), and Pre-Market High/Low (PMH/PML).
