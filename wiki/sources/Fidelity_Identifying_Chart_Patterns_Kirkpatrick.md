## 1. Foundational Tenets & Limits of Chart Patterns
- **Definition**: A pattern is bounded by at least two trend lines (straight or curved).
- **Activation Criterion**: A pattern is **not complete or activated until an actual breakout occurs**. Anticipating breakouts before confirmation invites severe whipsaws.
- **Fractal Nature**: Patterns behave consistently across all timeframes (weekly, daily, intraday).
- **Human Cognitive Traps**:
  - Seeing patterns where none exist (apophenia).
  - Believing "market lore" without statistical verification.
  - Clinging to initial price targets after market conditions have fundamentally shifted.

---

## 2. Advanced Execution & Confirmation Filters
Breakouts require confirmation before capital allocation:
1. **Intrabar Filter**: Price trades beyond boundary during the bar.
2. **Multiple Closes Filter**: Requiring 2 consecutive bar closes outside the boundary.
3. **Time Filter**: Remaining outside the breakout zone for a predefined time duration (e.g. 1-2 periods).
4. **Percentage / Point Filter**: Price clearing the level by a buffer (e.g. 1-3% or $1.00).
5. **Volume / Money Filter**: Significant surge in dollar volume verifying institutional participation.

---

## 3. False Breakouts vs. Failed Breakouts (Traps)
- **False Breakout**: Price breaks out but almost immediately returns back through its breakout price.
- **Failed Breakout (The Trap)**: False breakout occurs and price reverses violently in the opposite direction.
- **Stop and Reverse Strategy**:
  - Enter upon initial breakout.
  - Place protective stop outside the breakout bar.
  - Place a secondary entry stop at the exact same level (a "Stop and Reverse" order).
  - If the breakout succeeds, profit from the initial trade; if it fails, immediately profit from the sharp counter-trend move.

---

## 4. Retracement Mechanics: Pullbacks vs. Throwbacks
- **Throwback**: Upward breakout pulls back to test broken resistance as new support.
- **Pullback**: Downward breakout retraces to test broken support as new resistance.
- *Critical Warning*: Retracements do not always occur; waiting for them can cause missed runaway moves, and performance can suffer when they do occur.

## 5. Construction & Calculated Targets for Major Patterns
| Pattern | Geometry / Touch Rule | Price Target Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| **Double / Triple Top** | 2 or 3 peaks at resistance separated by troughs | Height of highest peak to trough, subtracted from breakout point |
| **Double / Triple Bottom** | 2 or 3 troughs at support separated by peaks | Distance from troughs to peak, added to breakout point |
| **Rectangles** | Support/resistance bounding range; "Shortfall" indicates eventual breakout direction | Height from resistance to support line added/subtracted to breakout |
| **Symmetrical Triangle** | At least 2 touches per bound; upper slopes down, lower slopes up | Height of widest point added/subtracted to breakout point |
| **Ascending Triangle** | Upper horizontal, lower rising; upward break more common | Height of pattern added to breakout |
| **Descending Triangle** | Lower horizontal, upper descending; downward break more common | Height of pattern subtracted from breakout |
| **Rising / Falling Wedges** | Both lines slope same direction; requires at least 5 touches (3 on one, 2 on other) | Target is lowest trough (for downward) or pattern height added |
| **Head & Shoulders (Top/Bottom)**| Center peak higher than shoulders; activated upon neckline break | Head-to-neckline distance projected from neckline breakout |
| **Cup and Handle (Saucer)** | Rounded base with two lips and handle | Height from right lip to cup bottom added to breakout |

## 6. Short-Term & Volatility Formations
- **Explosion Gap Pivot**: Gap up followed by throwback. If throwback does not fill the gap ("pivot low"), buy above the high of the gap bar with protective stop below the pivot low.
- **Pipe Bottom (Two-Bar Reversal)**: Two oversized weekly bars at end of a downtrend; second bar closes in the upper half. High reliability on weekly charts.
- **Narrow Range 4 (NR4) & Inside Bar**: Low volatility contraction precedes explosive expansion. Enter on breakout of the narrow bar's high/low.
