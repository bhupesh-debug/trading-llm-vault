# Source Summary: Mark Minervini Setup & VCP System

## Overview
- **Source**: Raw extracted notes from [[raw/document.pdf]]
- **Author/Methodology**: Mark Minervini (U.S. Investing Champion, author of *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard*)
- **Core Strategy**: Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP) coupled with strict Trend Templates and asymmetric risk sizing.

---

## 1. Stock Screening Criteria (The Minervini Trend Template)
- **Within 25% of 52-Week High**: Focus exclusively on market leaders displaying institutional demand.
- **Price > $30**: Disqualify low-liquidity penny stocks.
- **200 SMA Rising for ≥ 3 Months**: Non-negotiable long-term structural uptrend.
- **50 SMA > 200 SMA**: Intermediate trend accelerating above long-term trend.
- **Price > 200 SMA & preferably > 50 SMA**.
- **Price ≥ 100% off 52-Week Low**: Eliminates bottom-fishing traps.
- **Made a 52-Week High at least once every 4 to 6 months**: Demonstrates sustained institutional accumulation.

---

## 2. Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP) Mechanics
- **Weekly Chart Evaluation**: Use weekly charts for structure and price/volume accumulation; use daily charts for tactical entry.
- **Formula for Contraction**: 
  $$\text{Volatility Drop} = \left(\frac{\text{Swing Low}}{\text{Swing High}}\right) - 1$$
- **Ideal Pattern**: 3 sequential contractions (T1, T2, T3), with each successive price wave contracting volatility by roughly **50%**. (Take a 2-contraction VCP only if the 2nd contraction drops volatility by > 70%).
- **Time Contraction**: The duration between contraction waves also shortens alongside price amplitude.
- **Volume Footprint**: Heavy volume on up-swings; drying up / low volume on consolidations and pullbacks.
- **Buy Trigger**: Breakout through the pivot point when the previous swing high is taken out on expanding volume. Typical base duration: 4–8 weeks.

## 3. Position Sizing, Pyramiding & Risk Rules
- **Initial Risk**: **0.5%** capital at risk on the first trade (scaling to 1.0% on 2nd trade).
- **Stop Loss Calculation**:
  - Plot **20-period ATR** on the daily chart.
  - Set Stop Loss at **$2 \times \text{ATR}(20)$** below entry.
  - Hard Cap: **Maximum 10% stop loss** under any market condition.
  - Never lower a stop loss downward.
- **Pyramiding Protocol**:
  - Add to the winning position only when price is **> 5% up** from the initial buy price.
  - Move the 1st trade's stop loss to cost (**Breakeven**).
  - Treat every addition as a fresh trade with its own $2\times\text{ATR}$ stop loss.
- **Exits**:
  - Trailing ATR stop.
  - Sell into strength if the 10 DMA slope is too steep (80°–90° parabolic rise).
  - Exit if heavy volume distribution occurs or exhaustion gaps fail to hold.
  - **Time Stop**: If the stock fails to move after 15–20 days, cut the position to free up capital.

---

## 4. Drawdown Budget & Mindset
- **Monthly Drawdown Budget (5-6%)**: If total portfolio drawdown reaches 5–6% in a given month, close all positions and cease trading for the remainder of the month.
- **Expectancy Realism**: Win rate is typically ~50%. 4–5 out of 10 trades will generate all the profit; losses are merely the ordinary cost of doing business.
- **Douglas Alignment**: Accept that anything can happen; think in probabilities and treat every trade independently.
