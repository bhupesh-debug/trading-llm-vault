# Source Summary: Visual Chart Patterns Reference & Cheatsheet

This reference compiles high-resolution technical diagrams and structural classifications for all major classical chart patterns.

---

## 1. Primary Pattern Atlas

![[raw/assets/here_are_some_chart_patterns_to_keep_in_the_back.png]]

### Key Categorizations:
- **Reversal Formations**:
  - Double Top / Double Bottom
  - Head and Shoulders / Inverse Head and Shoulders
  - Rising Wedge (Bearish Reversal) / Falling Wedge (Bullish Reversal)
- **Continuation Formations**:
  - Bullish / Bearish Rectangles
  - Bullish / Bearish Flags & Pennants
  - Falling Wedge (Bullish Continuation) / Rising Wedge (Bearish Continuation)
- **Bilateral Formations**:
  - Symmetrical Triangle
  - Ascending Triangle (Upside bias, but can break downward)
  - Descending Triangle (Downside bias, but can break upward)

## 2. Advanced Microstructure & Target Map

![[raw/assets/Chart-Patterns-2-1.jpg]]

### Detailed Structural Mechanics:
- **Neckline Validation**: Must connect genuine reaction swing pivots; slanted necklines require confirmation on horizontal support/resistance breaks.
- **Fibonacci Extensions for Cup & Handle**: Take-profit targets aligned with 1.618 and 2.618 Fibonacci expansions.
- **Exotic Formations**:
  - **Diamond Top & Diamond Bottom**: Volatility expansion followed by rapid contraction.
  - **Bump and Run Pattern (BARR)**: Lead-in phase, sharp acceleration "bump", trendline break, and rundown to lead-in lows.
  - **Broadening Formation (Megaphone)**: Expanding higher highs and lower lows signaling extreme emotional instability and institutional distribution.
