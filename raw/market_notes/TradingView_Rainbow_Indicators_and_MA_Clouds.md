# Raw Notes: TradingView Rainbow Indicators & Moving Average Clouds

## Verbatim Overview
The TradingView Scripts Library features several popular "rainbow" technical and sentiment indicators, which layer multiple moving averages, bands, or color-coded zones to visualize momentum, trends, and market cycles.

### Common Types of Rainbow Indicators on TradingView:

1. **Rainbow Moving Averages (Rainbow MA Cloud / Rainbow Averages)**:
   - Uses a stack of 8 to 10 Moving Averages (often using Fibonacci sequences like 8, 13, 21, 34, 55, 89, 144, 233).
   - **Bullish Alignment**: Shorter MAs sit neatly above longer MAs, showing strong upward momentum.
   - **Bearish Alignment**: Shorter MAs sit below longer MAs, signaling downward pressure.
   - **Widening Ribbon**: Suggests trend acceleration, while a contracting or tangled ribbon points to consolidation.

2. **Rainbow Price Charts (e.g., Bitcoin/Ethereum Rainbow Charts)**:
   - Long-term, logarithmic overlay charts that divide historical price action into colored "sentiment bands" (such as Accumulation, Buy Zone, HODL, and Bubble/Peak zones).
   - Used primarily by long-term investors to gauge macro market cycles.

3. **Visual Rainbow Indicators (e.g., Rainbow Unicorn)**:
   - Aesthetic or custom-colored tools that cycle rainbow gradients across price bars, wicks, or chart borders for visual tracking without complex mathematical calculations.
