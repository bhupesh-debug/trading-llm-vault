# Top Profitable Chart Patterns & Historical Success Rates

![[raw/assets/top_profitable_chart_patterns.jpg]]

## High-Reliability Chart Patterns

| Chart Pattern | Classification | Historical Success Rate | Average Gain | Key Characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Inverse Head & Shoulders** | Bullish Reversal | **~89%** | **~45%** | Triple bottoming structure with a lower center trough; breaks above neckline with volume. |
| **Double Bottom** | Bullish Reversal | **~88%** | **~50%** | "W" shape; retests support floor successfully before breaking resistance. |
| **Triple Bottom** | Bullish Reversal | **~87%** | **~45%** | Three distinct touches of support; strong institutional accumulation. |
| **Descending Triangle** | Bullish Reversal / Breakout | **~87%** | **~38%** | Lower highs pushing against flat horizontal support, breaking upward violently. |
| **Rectangle Top** | Consolidation / Continuation | **~85%** | **~51%** | Horizontal bounds; institutional re-accumulation leading to expansion. |
| **Rectangle Bottom** | Reversal / Consolidation | **~85%** | **~48%** | Floor consolidation range resolving upward. |
| **Bull Flag** | Bullish Continuation | **~85%** | **~39%** | Sharp impulse pole followed by downward-sloping consolidation channel. |
| **Ascending Triangle** | Bullish Continuation | **~83%** | **~43%** | Rising higher lows against flat horizontal resistance; eventual breakout. |
| **Rising Wedge** | Reversal / Breakdown | **~81%** | **~38%** | Contracting upward channel showing exhausting volume before a breakdown. |
| **Cup and Handle** | Bullish Continuation | **High Reliability** | Varies | Rounded accumulation base followed by shallow pullback handle. |

## Patterns to Avoid (Low Reliability)

| Pattern | Historical Success Rate | Average Gain | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pennant Patterns** | **~46%** | **~7%** | **AVOID / Disqualified**: Worse than a coin toss. Highly prone to false breakouts, whip-saws, and low reward-to-risk ratio. |
