# Face2Face Transcript: Systematic Options & Algo Trading Architecture (Keshav & Vivek Bajaj)

## Verbatim Core Transcript
Investments in the securities market are subject to market risk. Read all the related documents carefully before investing. First thing — options trading, in intraday trading, gives you an edge if you're knowledgeable about it. Otherwise, doing intraday trading and being profitable — unless you're an operator or an insider — is very difficult, impossible. Jane Street hired freshers from IIT Madras on four-crore packages — the whole of India goes crazy over it. But if a senior from that same IIT Madras sits down, writes this algo and trades with it, we call him a gambler.

Retail traders lost 70,000 crore, 9,000 crore, 1 lakh crore — 10 billion dollars. In India the term-insurance market is worth 30,000 crore, and every year people let that go. If I'm losing 30,000 crore on life insurance and 30,000 crore on my wealth, both should be seen the same way. It was my niece's wedding that day. That same day India's trade-deal announcement came out and caused the biggest gap-up ever — and my algo was short. I lost three-four percent of whatever capital I had put in.

SEBI is a regulator, it helps you too — but every step SEBI has taken in the last one, one and a half, two years has made this a loss-making game. There was a city where a lot of theft used to happen. The commissioner had a problem — too much theft. So he said: tell everyone not to leave the house, and if they do go out, go out in a BMW, wear ten kilos of gold, and carry cash in your pocket too. Wow, right? If there's going to be theft, let it be big theft — why small theft? I also know I need peace — I don't want money that comes without peace of mind. On my birthday I'd taken my family out for dinner, and that same day the stop-loss jumped and I lost a lakh just sitting there. So that day I shut it — no, I don't want to do this. There are two important women in my life — one is my Home Minister, one is our Finance Minister. Both of them think we're gambling.
