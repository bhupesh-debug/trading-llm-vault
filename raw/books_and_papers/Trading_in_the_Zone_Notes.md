# Raw Notes: Trading in the Zone by Mark Douglas

Key notes extracted from reading:
- 95% of trading errors come from our attitudes about: being wrong, losing money, missing out, and leaving money on the table.
- An edge is just a higher probability of one thing happening over another.
- The market cannot hurt you unless you allow your expectations to betray reality.
- Execute trades mechanically over blocks of 20-25 trades before judging whether a setup works.
